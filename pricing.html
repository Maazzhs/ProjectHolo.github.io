Common questions

Is Holo really available right now? ▶
The core voice engine is live and being tested with early access partners. Scheduling and CRM features are in active development. Sign up to get access as each module ships.
What counts as a "call"? ▶
Any inbound interaction handled by Holo — voice call, SMS thread, or web chat session. Outbound reminders sent by Holo do not count toward your limit.
Can I change plans later? ▶
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. No penalties either way.
Do you offer a free trial? ▶
Early access partners get a 14-day trial on the Pro plan, no credit card required. Reach out via the early access form to get in the queue.
What happens to my data? ▶
Call logs and transcripts are stored encrypted and are only accessible by your account. We do not use your data to train models. Full privacy policy coming soon.
